Standard N-slit grating diffraction, I(x) — Fraunhofer (far-field)

This is the textbook multi-slit diffraction-grating formula, coded independently of the exact-path (Fresnel) equation. N grooves of spacing 1/Λ are illuminated at angle α to the grating surface; the screen sits a perpendicular distance d₀ away, with the grating surface tilted by β from the screen's normal. In the far field the phase from groove to groove grows linearly with groove index n, so the coherent sum collapses to the closed-form grating function below.

φ(x) = (2π/λΛ)·[ (d₀cosβ − x·sinβ)/√(d₀²+x²) − cosα ]

I(x) = A₀² · [ sin((N+1)φ/2) / sin(φ/2) ]²
